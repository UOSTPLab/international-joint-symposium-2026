## About the Event

The International Joint Symposium & Workshop on Transit-Oriented Development 2026 brings together researchers, faculty members, and students from universities and institutions around the world to explore the future of transit-oriented cities.

Held at the University of Seoul from August 2 to 12, 2026, the event examines how high-speed rail, artificial intelligence, emerging mobility systems, and urban technologies are reshaping cities and metropolitan regions.

Through academic lectures, technical site visits, collaborative workshops, and team-based projects, participants will investigate key challenges and opportunities in transit-oriented development. Seoul will serve as a living laboratory in which academic knowledge can be connected with real-world urban and transportation systems.

## Program Overview

The eleven-day program combines an international symposium with an intensive collaborative workshop.

The symposium features lectures by experts in transit-oriented development, transportation planning, artificial intelligence, urban robotics, high-speed rail, and future mobility. During the workshop, students from participating universities will form international teams, participate in technical site visits, conduct collaborative analysis, and develop proposals addressing contemporary urban and transportation challenges.

The program concludes with final team presentations, discussions, and the submission of team outputs.

## Key Themes

### Transit-Oriented Development

Planning compact, accessible, and sustainable urban environments around high-capacity public transport systems.

### AI and Intelligent Mobility

Applying artificial intelligence, data analytics, and digital technologies to transportation and urban planning.

### High-Speed Rail and Urban Transformation

Examining how high-speed rail reshapes regional accessibility, station areas, and metropolitan development.

### Robotics and Future Cities

Exploring the role of automation, autonomous mobility, and urban robotics in future urban environments.
