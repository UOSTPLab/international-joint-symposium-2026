## About the Symposium and Workshop

The International Joint Symposium & Workshop on Transit-Oriented Development 2026 will be held in Seoul, Korea, from August 2 to 12, 2026. The event brings together international researchers and students to explore the future of cities shaped by high-speed rail, artificial intelligence, and emerging mobility systems.

The main program includes academic lectures, technical tours, collaborative workshops, and team-based presentations. Seoul will serve as a living case study for examining the relationship between transportation systems, urban development, and emerging technologies.
